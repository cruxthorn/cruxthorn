## About Me

Hi everyone, I'm **Cruxthorn**, an Offensive Vulnerability Researcher.

My story began in primary school, when I was first introduced to online games. At first, I was curious about **"how to make playing games easier"**. I even became a cheater in various online games. But that curiosity soon grew into **"how do you actually build a cheat?"**

Through that exploration, I discovered the world of hacking and began teaching myself in junior high school. My interest in becoming a **black hat hacker** emerged after coming across various pieces of content online.

I also thought, and genuinely felt, that **"If you can hack, you can rule the world"** would hold true in the future. For a while I took a wrong turn into Front-end Development because I didn't yet know the roadmap, until I realised that my interest still lay on the offensive side.

In senior high school, during the course of my learning, I also carried out **unauthorised access and exfiltration** for personal satisfaction. However, as my understanding grew, I came to see that such skills must be used responsibly.

Eventually, I discovered Bug Bounty Programs, which opened up an opportunity to apply my skills while earning money from the vulnerabilities I found. From there, I developed a desire to discover a **zero-day vulnerability**.

My dream is simple, but not easy: to become an Offensive Vulnerability Researcher. I'm drawn to the offensive field because I find it incredibly cool and highly relevant for the future.

My name comes from an idea: **Crux** represents the core, or critical point, of a problem, while **Thorn** symbolises sharpness and the ability to pierce defences. So **Cruxthorn** stands for sharpness in finding, and breaking through, the weakest point of a system.
